You may be wondering why anyone would build this. Here is the honest answer.

## 01 · the pain

### I really hate setting up a dev machine.

Most people around me have heard me say it: **I hate setting up my development environment.** Every new PC meant hours of searching for how to install each tool and package, and I almost never remembered the steps from last time.

By the time everything was ready, I was often too bored to start the project I sat down to build. Sometimes I never came back to it.

## 02 · why now

### I never thought of building it, until my machine was compromised.

My LinkedIn account was hacked recently. While investigating my work PC, I found **malware running on it without my knowledge.** The only safe option was to reset the laptop and start clean.

The irony: I had just finished setting that laptop up. After the reset I had to do it all over again, and I couldn't remember most of the packages, tools and VS Code extensions I had installed. It was slow and frustrating.

## 03 · the question

### How do I get the same environment back?

That is when I asked myself: **what if I have to reset again, change PCs, or switch operating systems?** How do I get the exact same environment without relying on my memory?

dkit is my answer. Scan your setup, back it up encrypted, and restore it anywhere, on Windows, macOS or Linux. That is how `devsetupkit-cli`, or `dkit` for short, began.

---

I hope it saves you the weekend it would have cost me.
